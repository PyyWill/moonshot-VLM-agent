1. Start the assembly by using the [Lumenier_QAV-S_2_Joshua_Bardwell_SE_Split_Rear_Plate] to mount the [Lumenier_QAV-S_2_Joshua_Bardwell_Aluminum_X-Lock] with the M3x22mm screws and the M3x6mm screws.
2. Secure the [Lumenier_QAV-S_2_Joshua_Bardwell_SE_5_inch_Arm] by tightening the [Lumenier_QAV-S_2_Joshua_Bardwell_Aluminum_Arm_Wedge_5mm] using the M3x16mm screws to lock them in place.
3. Add the [Lumenier_QAV-S_2_Joshua_Bardwell_SE_Split_Front_Plate] to the assembly and use the M3x6mm screw in the middle to secure it.
4. Use the M3x6mm button head screws to secure the [Lumenier_QAV-S_2_Joshua_Bardwell_Knurled_Standoff] and the [Lumenier_QAV-S_2_Joshua_Bardwell_Aluminum_FPV_Camera_Mounts] to the [Lumenier_QAV-S_2_Joshua_Bardwell_SE_Split_Front_Plate]. Use the M3x16mm screws through the [Lumenier_QAV-S_2_Joshua_Bardwell_SE_5_inch_Arm] into the [Lumenier_QAV-S_2_Joshua_Bardwell_Knurled_Standoff].
5. Secure the [Lumenier_QAV-S_2_Joshua_Bardwell_SE_Top_Plate] to the assembly by using the M3x6mm button head screws in the front and the M3x6mm countersink head screws in the back.
6. Add the GoPro mount and the spacer plate together with a M3x8mm screw and finish the build by sticking the battery pad and the strap.
